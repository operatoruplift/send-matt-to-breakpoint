# RVAClassic visual identity

Applied October 23, 2026 from Matt’s supplied logo and brand-sheet references.

The portfolio uses Lavender `#C3A1F2`, White `#F4F4F6`, Obsidian `#0A0A0C`, and Graphite `#1F1F23`. Use Purple `#8551BF` with white text for primary buttons. On light surfaces, use `#7239A8` for small purple text and interactive indicators. Keep external client and publication logos in their native colors.

The standalone brand kit uses Space Grotesk and Saira. Its font files and OFL licenses are in `dist/portfolio/brand/fonts/`. The portfolio retains its established Kanit body/display typography and the original colorful hero with DM Sans, Playfair Display, and Poppins. Keep brand palette changes scoped to the portfolio body; do not recolor or desaturate the hero scene.

The supplied classical bust, circle, pixel grid, and star form the identity. Primary and monochrome logos are true vector paths. Preserve the circle and proportions; leave clear space. Use the small ICO/PNG exports for favicons and the primary SVG for larger display.

Tagline: **Build. Compete. Create.** Voice: direct, concrete, and specific about the work. Keep competition prize pools distinct from personal winnings, and team outcomes distinct from individual placements. Avoid invented urgency, guarantees, and social metrics.
